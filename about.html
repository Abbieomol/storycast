About StoryCast

Our mission

StoryCast shares short, true stories from Kenya in audio and video. Each story is built so everyone can enjoy it, whether you watch, listen or read, and whether you use a mouse, a keyboard or a screen reader.

The stories are retold from published Kenyan reporting, and we credit our sources on every story page.

Accessibility features

Captions

Every video has captions you can switch on and off. We use high-contrast text to ensure legibility.

Transcripts

Every story has a full text transcript, including descriptions of what is on screen for low-vision users.

Keyboard navigation

Everything works with Tab, Enter and Space, with a clear focus outline for keyboard-only users.

Readable colour

Text and background colours meet the WCAG 2.1 AA contrast standard (4.5:1 ratio or higher).

Screen reader support

Landmarks, headings and labels help screen readers move around the page logically and quickly.

Reduced motion

No autoplay, and animations switch off automatically if your device asks for less motion.

How to engage

  1. 01. Choose a story from our curated collection.
  2. 02. Turn on captions, or open the transcript to follow along.
  3. 03. Read the sources behind the story for deeper context.

Editorial note: Stories on this website are retellings of published stories. The clinic story is informational and not medical advice. The mountain story is not a recommended itinerary. The website owners are not liable for any actions taken using information acquired from it.
